AutoJs6 mobile touch overlay - third-party notices

The scripts in this directory were written for AutoJs6 from a behavior specification
(tools/ace-mobile/README.md in the plugin repository). Two parts follow published algorithms:

1. autojs6_mobile_scroller.js

   The spline fling interpolation follows android.widget.Scroller of the Android Open Source
   Project, adapted to JavaScript and modified (single combined fling, positions clamped to the
   bounds, no flywheel).
